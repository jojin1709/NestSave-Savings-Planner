NestSave is a deterministic, 100% offline-first personal savings planner and wealth roadmap engine designed for maximum privacy and zero cloud dependence.

Your savings balances, financial targets, and transactions are stored entirely on your device in an encrypted Room SQLite database. No accounts, no servers, no bank scraping, and zero tracking.

Key Capabilities:
• 100% Offline Architecture: Runs without internet connection. Zero tracking scripts, zero ads, zero telemetry.
• Hardware Biometric Lock: Secure your vault behind native fingerprint or 3D facial recognition authentication.
• Indian Banking Calculators: Mathematical precision for Recurring Deposits (quarterly compounding formula matching SBI/HDFC/ICICI) and Fixed Deposits.
• 1-Click Plan Recalibration: Algorithmic shortfall recovery to adjust deadlines or monthly deposits when life changes.
• Salary-Day Batch Allocator: Distribute monthly savings across multiple active goals in one tap based on priority weighting.
• Milestone Streaks & Badges: Stay motivated with daily streaks and unlocked achievements.
• Client-Side Vault Backups: Export and restore password-protected AES-GCM 256 backup archives.
• Android 15 & 16 KB Page Aligned: Modern Linux ELF binaries optimized for 120Hz smooth performance.
